How to play

Solve three riddles a day. They get harder as you go: easy, medium, then hard.

Each riddle has 60 seconds on the clock. The sand starts falling once the riddle has appeared.

You have three lives for the whole day. A wrong answer snuffs out a candle. If the sand runs out, that riddle is lost, but you keep your lives.

Stuck? A hint unlocks with 30 seconds left.
